Model assumes well-mixed air, a constant air-change rate, and temperature and moisture moving exponentially toward outdoor conditions. It does not account for heating, thermal mass of walls/furnishings, solar gain, moisture released by surfaces, or changing airflow. Temperature prediction is particularly simplified; use as a guide, not a guarantee.

How to use the result

Lower outdoor absolute humidity: ventilation will generally dry the air. Consider the predicted RH and temperature together when deciding how long to ventilate.

Higher outdoor absolute humidity: ventilation may add moisture. Continue necessary fresh-air ventilation regardless.
