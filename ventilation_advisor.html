How to use the result

Lower outdoor absolute humidity: airing the room will generally remove moisture. Brief, wide-open ventilation is often more effective than leaving a window slightly open for a long time.

Higher outdoor absolute humidity: ventilation may add moisture. Consider waiting for drier conditions, while still maintaining necessary fresh-air ventilation.

This compares moisture per cubic metre at the measured temperatures. It assumes similar air pressure and does not predict the exact final room humidity: the result also depends on ventilation rate, room temperature, furnishings, and indoor moisture sources.
